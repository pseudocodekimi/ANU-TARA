About aNU Tara?

“aNU TARA?” is a web-based food discovery and recommendation system designed explicitly for students of National University - Manila. The concept originated directly from a common point of frustration experienced within our community: when friends frequently ask each other, "Saan Tayo Kakain?" during short class break windows.

With limited time, varying budgets, and differing food preferences, turning routine choices into decisions can be tough. By aggregating student-generated reviews, ratings, and active mood filters, we clear the path to the best localized dining options surrounding the campus.
